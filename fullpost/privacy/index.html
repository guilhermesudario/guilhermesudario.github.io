Privacy Policy — Fullpost

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Policy explains how the Fullpost app ("app") handles information. It follows Brazil's General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018, "LGPD") and also applies to users in other countries, including the European Union (GDPR).

Controller: Guilherme Amancio Sudario, individual.
Privacy contact: gs.studio.apps@gmail.com

1. Summary

  • The app does not require sign-up or login.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To do this, the AdMob SDK collects some device data (see section 4). We ourselves do not collect or sell personal data.
  • The photos and videos you use are processed only on your device and are never sent to us, to Google or to anyone else.

2. Photos and videos

To build a post, the app uses the system photo picker: it receives only the items you choose, not your whole library. To save the finished post, the app asks for permission to add items to your photo library. You can remove this permission at any time in your device settings.

Temporary files created while building the post stay in the app's area on the device and are cleared by the system. They are also removed when you delete the app.

3. Sharing

When you tap "Post to Instagram" or "Share", the file goes to the app you choose and is then subject to that app's privacy policy.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads from Google AdMob (Google LLC). A full-screen ad may appear after you export a post. To choose and measure ads, the AdMob SDK collects and sends to Google data such as: IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device model and operating system, device identifiers, and your interactions with the ads. Google handles this data under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

  • Personalized ads. On iPhone, the app asks for your permission (Apple's "tracking" prompt) before using the advertising identifier. If you don't allow it, ads still appear, but they are not personalized. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • European Union, European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland. When you open the app, Google's consent form appears so you can choose what you accept. To review your choice, tap the hand icon at the top of the screen.
  • Your photos and videos are never sent to Google: ads have no access to them.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children. Ads follow Google's rules for content suitable for all ages.

6. Your rights

The LGPD and the GDPR give you, among others, the rights to confirm, access, correct and delete your data. We do not keep any personal data about you; ad data stays with Google, which offers controls at https://myadcenter.google.com. Questions: gs.studio.apps@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy if the app gets new features. The date of the last update is always shown at the top.

8. Governing law

This Policy is governed by Brazilian law.
